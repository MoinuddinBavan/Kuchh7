# kuchhe7 v1.0 — five-minute client walkthrough

Keep the application maximized at 1920 × 1080. Save/export only into the prepared
demo output folder. The engineering and nesting rules are feasibility examples.

1. Click **Complete** on the ribbon to load the six-unit kitchen in one action.
2. In **Design Studio**, point out the dimensioned 2D room, door/window and six
   coordinated cabinet footprints.
3. Enable **Present**, click **Camera**, and show the premium 3D room, connected
   countertop, sink, two wall units and tall pantry.
4. Select C1 and change Width from 800 mm to 700 mm. Show the isolated 2D, 3D and
   BOM update. This valid reduction avoids a placement collision.
5. Open **Manufacturing Preview** and show the labeled exploded assembly.
6. Select **Door** to show its dimensioned component drawing and BOM-derived data.
7. Open **Drilling Preview** and state that the adjustable holes are illustrative
   and are never exported as machining data.
8. Open **Sheet Nesting**, choose a material/thickness group, switch sheets, click
   a part, and point out eligible/placed/unplaced area plus allocated-sheet
   utilization. Click **Recalculate nesting** after the dimension edit.
9. Export **BOM (XLSX)** and **Elevation (DXF)**. State explicitly that the DXF is
   a demonstration cabinet front elevation, not a nesting layout, toolpath or
   machine cutting instruction.

Close by stating that this is a client-approval feasibility prototype, not a
factory-approved manufacturing system.
